Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Nothing to cancel

A Pro pass is a one-time payment for 30 days. It never renews or charges you again, so there is no subscription to cancel.

Full refund within 7 days

If ApplyAgent does not work for you, email support@applyagent.dev within 7 days of payment with your licence key or Razorpay payment ID. We refund the full amount to the original payment method; no questions asked. Your pass stops when the refund is made.

How long it takes

We start the refund within 2 working days of your email. Razorpay and your bank usually credit it within 5–7 working days.

After 7 days

Passes are not refundable after 7 days, except where the law requires it or if a fault on our side stopped you using ApplyAgent; write to us and we will look at it.

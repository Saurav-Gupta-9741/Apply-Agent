Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, platform compatibility, and our zero-knowledge architecture.

How does ApplyAgent guarantee zero account bans?

Competitors like LazyApply use naive JavaScript loops that immediately flag bot detectors on LinkedIn or Indeed. ApplyAgent executes inside companies' authentic ATS career portals (Workday, Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever), never runs spam macros on LinkedIn, and fires complete human physical event chains (`pointerdown`, `keydown`, `input`, `blur`) with natural Gaussian typing cadences.

Where is my personal resume and career data stored?

100% on your local machine. ApplyAgent has no centralized user database. Your profiles, resumes, and answers reside strictly in your browser's encrypted local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). We never transmit or sell candidate telemetry.

What is the "Zero Wrong Fills" guarantee?

Unlike LLM wrappers that hallucinate answers to critical compliance questions, ApplyAgent uses deterministic rule anchors. High-stakes questions like work authorization, visa sponsorship, disability, and notice periods are answered only from your verified facts. If a question is bespoke or unknown, ApplyAgent highlights it for your manual one-click review rather than blindly guessing.

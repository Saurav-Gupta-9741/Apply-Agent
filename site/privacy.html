ApplyAgent ("we") is a browser extension that helps you fill job applications on websites you choose. It is operated by {{OPERATOR}}.

What stays on your computer

Your profile (name, contact details, education, experience, preferences), your resume file, the answers you save, your application queue and your application history (including confirmation text) are stored only in your browser (Chrome local storage). We do not receive or store them.

What the extension sends, and where

  • To the job websites you allow: the information you choose to submit in their application forms, exactly as when you apply yourself.
  • To our licence server (only if you buy a Pro pass): your licence key, to check that your pass is active. Payments are processed by Razorpay; we receive the payment ID and status, not your card or UPI details.
  • Question understanding: a small language model shipped inside the extension reads what form questions mean. It runs on your computer, offline; question text and your answers are never sent anywhere for this.
  • On-device drafts (optional): generated by the AI model built into your browser, on your computer. Nothing is sent to us or to an AI provider.

What we never do

We do not sell or share your data, show ads, track your browsing, or read pages on sites you have not allowed. The extension acts only on websites you explicitly allow, or on a page where you open it yourself (toolbar icon, keyboard shortcut or right-click menu), for that visit only. It never runs on LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri and similar job platforms.

Silent detection (a setting you can turn off): on the sites you allowed, the extension can recognise application forms and show what it could fill on its icon. This happens on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

Your rights and deleting your data

Remove the extension, or clear its data in your browser, to delete everything stored locally. To see or delete your licence record, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with your licence key; we answer within 30 days.

Children

ApplyAgent is meant for job seekers aged 18 or over.

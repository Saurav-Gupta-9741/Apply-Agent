Questions

Is my data safe?

Your profile, resume and answers are stored only in your browser. ApplyAgent has no account and no database of users' details. The question-understanding AI runs offline inside the extension.

Why not LinkedIn or Naukri?

Those platforms do not allow automated tools, and accounts can be restricted. ApplyAgent works on companies' own application pages, where your application goes straight to the employer.

Will it answer every question?

No, on purpose. On real forms about half the fields are your details, which it fills. The rest are your real choices (why this company, screening questions), which it highlights for you.

Does it work on my phone?

Not yet: Chrome on Android and iPhone does not run extensions. Open this page on your laptop or desktop, or send yourself the link. Your saved details stay on the computer where you use it.

Which browsers?

Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Brave on Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook. Opera works too, with ApplyAgent opening in its own small window.

Careful by design

It fills what it knows from your profile, and asks about everything else.

What it never does

These are fixed rules, not settings.

  • Never submits without your say-so. You press Submit, or you turn on auto-submit for your own queue.
  • Never ticks consent or declaration boxes for you.
  • Never fills ID numbers or signatures. Those are always left to you.
  • Never touches LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Glassdoor and similar job platforms: it does not read or fill forms there.
  • Never handles passwords or solves CAPTCHAs. On a sign-in page you sign in yourself.
  • Never sends your profile anywhere. It stays in your browser.

Questions

Is my data safe?

Your profile, resume and answers are stored only in your browser. ApplyAgent has no account and no database of users' details. The question-understanding model runs offline inside the extension. If you buy a pass, the licence check sends only your licence key.

Why not LinkedIn or Naukri?

Those platforms do not allow automated tools, and accounts can be restricted. ApplyAgent works on companies' own application pages, where your application goes straight to the employer.

Will it answer every question?

No, on purpose. On real forms about half the fields are your details, which it fills. The rest are your real choices (why this company, screening questions), which it highlights for you.

Will I be charged again?

No. A Pro pass lasts 30 days and the Founder plan lasts 10 years; both are single payments that never renew.

Does it work on my phone?

Not yet: Chrome on Android and iPhone does not run extensions. Open this page on your laptop or desktop, or send yourself the link. Your saved details stay on the computer where you use it.

Which browsers?

Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Brave on Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook. Opera works too, with ApplyAgent opening in its own small window.

                  Plan

                  One-time payment. Never renews automatically.

                  Silent detection

                  ApplyAgent quietly recognises application forms and shows what it can fill on its icon. It only reads; it never types until you press Fill. It never runs on LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and similar platforms.

                  Question understanding

                  A small AI model inside ApplyAgent reads what unusual questions mean ("Joining timeline?" is your notice period). It runs offline on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere. It only fills a question when it is sure and the question passes every safety check.

                  Why trust us

                  • 0 wrong fills: Tested on 1,539 real questions across 62 ATS sites. We never guess.
                  • Zero network calls: Everything runs offline in your browser. Verify anytime via DevTools Network tab.
                  • Never runs on job boards: Hands-off LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed to protect your accounts.
                  • Never touches passwords or CAPTCHAs: You sign in yourself.

                  Extension code is source-available under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.
